💡

Varale Community Road Safety Tips

• Maintain safe speed (< 30 km/h) near ZP Primary School Road and residential areas.
• Watch out for water-filled potholes near Varale Phata Junction during heavy rainfall.
• Heavy industrial vehicles frequently commute along the Talegaon-Chakan Link Road; two-wheeler riders are advised to wear certified helmets and use headlights at dawn/dusk.
• In case of emergency road hazards, alert the local Gram Panchayat or community helpline at 02114-220000.
